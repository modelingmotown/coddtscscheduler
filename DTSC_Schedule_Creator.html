Scheduling Rules

  • Supervisors are assigned only from the designated supervisor pool.
  • Two different eligible counter employees are assigned per open weekday, and they must be from different teams/departments (for example, GIS + Sales—not Sales + Sales).
  • If no eligible cross-team pair exists, the generator leaves the second counter assignment blank and flags the day for review rather than violating the team rule.
  • Supervisors cannot be assigned to counter or break/lunch duty.
  • Break/lunch coverage must be a different employee from both counter assignments.
  • Counter assignments are capped at 2 per employee per month.
  • Break/lunch assignments are capped at 1 per employee per month.
  • No employee should receive counter duty and break/lunch duty in the same Monday–Friday week.
  • Counter assignments are limited to one per employee per week where feasible.
  • Monthly workload preference: aim for either two full counter shifts or one full counter shift plus one break/lunch shift, where staffing allows.
  • Avoid triple assignments: strongly penalize giving someone two full counter shifts plus a break/lunch shift in the same month; the generator will prefer other available staff.
  • Normally in-office staff are prioritized. If a normally WFH person is selected to fill a role, the day is flagged for review.
  • Approved time off and daily Unavailable/WFH changes remove an employee from consideration for that date.
  • Assignment balancing uses prior assignment counts to spread work as evenly as possible.

The generator prioritizes these rules. If staffing makes a complete assignment impossible, it leaves a REVIEW/CONFLICT warning instead of hiding the issue.
